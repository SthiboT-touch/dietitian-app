1 Executive Summary

1.1 Organization Overview

The client is a leading dietician practice with 16 branches across 9 provinces and a client base
exceeding 120,000 individuals. The organization has achieved significant success through per-
sonalized care, strong client relationships, and a trusted brand reputation built over years of
dedicated service.

1.2 Current State

Despite remarkable success in client acquisition and retention, the organization's operational
infrastructure remains entirely manual and paper-based. All core business processes--including
client registration, appointment scheduling, dietary plan management, progress tracking, billing,
and reporting--are conducted using physical documents and manual workflows.

1.3 Critical Challenges

The current operational model presents five critical challenges that threaten the organization's
ability to scale, maintain compliance, and deliver modern client experiences:
1. Broken First Impression: Manual, paper-based onboarding creates friction and diminishes

   the professional image
2. Operational Paralysis: Inaccessible information, time wastage, POPIA compliance failures,

   and zero business intelligence
3. Motivation Gap: Disconnected client journey between appointments leading to high churn

   rates
4. Revenue Stagnation: Single revenue stream dependency and geographic limitations
5. Feedback Black Hole: No systematic client satisfaction measurement or loyalty programs

1.4 Proposed Solution

This project proposes a comprehensive digital transformation using a unified Microsoft Power
Platform solution. Rather than implementing disparate systems, we will develop an integrated
Power Apps application with role-based access control (RBAC) that addresses all five problem
areas through interconnected modules provisioned based on user roles and branch requirements.

1.5 Strategic Vision

Transform the organization from a traditional, paper-based practice into a technology-driven
wellness ecosystem that:
· Delivers seamless digital experiences across all client touchpoints
· Enables data-driven decision making through centralized analytics
· Ensures regulatory compliance with POPIA and healthcare standards
· Supports scalable growth across geographic and service boundaries
· Creates multiple revenue streams through digital service delivery
· Maintains and enhances the personal care ethos that defines the brand

2.2 Problem Statement Summary

2.2.1 Problem 1: The Broken First Impression (Onboarding & Registration)
The client's first interaction with the practice establishes a poor foundation:
· Client Friction: Paper forms create a tedious, outdated experience that contradicts the

   premium positioning
· Administrative Burden: Manual data entry from handwritten forms causes delays and

   errors
· Lost Engagement: No digital touchpoint between registration and first appointment creates

   relationship gaps
· Brand Disconnect: Manual processes undermine the professional, modern image expected

   by clients

2.2.2 Problem 2: Operational Paralysis & Pervasive Risk
Managing 120,000 client files manually has created systemic operational failures:
· Inaccessible Information: Files locked to physical locations prevent cross-branch service

   and remote access
· Severe Time Wastage: Hours spent weekly on document retrieval, filing, and searching
· POPIA Compliance Failure: Physical files vulnerable to loss, damage, unauthorized access,

   and breaches
· Zero Business Intelligence: No ability to analyze demographics, track outcomes, or measure

   performance
· Collaboration Barriers: Inability to share insights or coordinate care across branches

2.2.3 Problem 3: The Motivation Gap (Disconnected Client Journey)
Value delivery limited to 60-minute consultations leaves clients unsupported:
· High Client Churn: Without continuous engagement, adherence drops and clients abandon

   programs

4 Unified Solution Architecture

4.1 Core Concept: One Platform, Multiple Modules

Rather than deploying separate applications for each problem area, this solution implements a
single, unified Power Apps platform with integrated modules that work together seamlessly.
The platform uses Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and device provisioning to ensure users
access only the features relevant to their role and location.
